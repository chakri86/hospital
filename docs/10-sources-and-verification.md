## Corrections to the initial conversational outline

1. “India has no data capture mechanism” is inaccurate; S-01 demonstrates an existing national mechanism. Actual facility-level gaps need field evidence.
2. The initial expense table did not form a precise quotation or internally reconciled budget. This repository replaces it with explicit editable assumptions and generated totals.
3. A year-end customer count multiplied by annual price overstates recognized revenue during growth. The monthly model accounts for additions and churn.
4. ABDM infrastructure and profile compatibility do not grant access to all patient records, guarantee certification or authorize research.
5. Removing names does not automatically make data anonymous, and consent withdrawal does not automatically override legal retention.
6. A convenience sample from private hospitals cannot support unqualified statewide disease-prevalence claims.

## Updating the evidence

Record source title, publisher, publication/version date, access date, precise supported claim and unresolved limitation. For legal determinations, also retain the applicable provision, commencement date, reviewer and signed conclusion privately. Do not present blog summaries as official certification requirements. Market prices and cloud quotations must include scope and date before replacing assumptions.
